🏠 Markets closest to the apartment

Distances are straight-line from Aspangstraße 12 (1030). Tap "Directions" for the actual walking or public-transport route.

1.2km

K&K Christmas Market, Belvedere open till 31 Dec

📅 20 Nov – 31 Dec🕚 Mon–Fri 11–21 · Sat, Sun & holidays 10–21 · 24 & 31 Dec until 17:00

Imperial-themed market in the palace park with traditional music, crafts and food. The closest market to the apartment and the only one of the official city markets open until New Year's Eve.

Directions ↗

Some guides report the market area has moved within the park this year. Check on site.

1.8km

Weihnachtszauber, Schwarzenbergplatz New 2026

📅 13 Nov – 26 Dec🕚 Mon–Fri 11–21 · Sat & Sun 10–21 · 24–26 Dec 11–16

Brand-new market, running for the first time this year, set around the Hochstrahlbrunnen fountain. Crafts, Austrian food, a stage programme and live music.

Directions ↗
2.1km

Art Advent, Karlsplatz

📅 13 Nov – 23 Dec🕚 Daily 12–20 · food & drinks until 21

Arts-and-crafts market in front of the Karlskirche. All food and drink stalls are certified organic. Good for families: straw play area and children's programme.

Directions ↗
2.7km

Christmas Market, Stephansplatz

📅 6 Nov – 26 Dec🕚 Daily 11–21 · 24 Dec 11–16 · 25 & 26 Dec 11–19

Small market directly at St. Stephen's Cathedral, focused on Austrian products. Easy to combine with a walk through the old town.

Directions ↗

🎄 Good to know

  • Entry is freeYou only pay for what you eat, drink or buy.
  • Mug depositPunsch and Glühwein come in a mug with a deposit. Return it to get the deposit back, or keep it as a souvenir.
  • Check 24 DecemberMost markets close early on Christmas Eve or are already closed. Check the times above before you go.
  • Two in one walkSchwarzenbergplatz and Karlsplatz are right next to each other and on the way to the city centre.

Dates and hours are taken from the City of Vienna market office announcement and organiser and tourism websites. Organisers can still change times, especially around the holidays, so please check before you go.
